
# Ethical Notes

This project is designed to support learners, not label them.

The risk score should never be used as an automatic decision-making tool. It should be treated as a prompt for human review and supportive follow-up.

## Key principles

- Data should be used to identify where support may be needed.
- A high risk score does not mean a learner is unwilling or unable to engage.
- Staff should consider context, barriers and learner voice before taking action.
- SEND, health, anxiety, caring responsibilities, transport, timetable issues and confidence may all affect engagement.
- The aim is early support, not punishment.

## Safeguards

Any dashboard or report based on this data should:

- avoid judgemental language
- limit access to appropriate staff
- use clear definitions
- show the reason behind each risk score
- allow staff to override or update the risk level based on professional judgement
- record actions taken after concerns are identified

## Example supportive actions

- tutor check-in
- parent or carer contact
- timetable adjustment
- SEND review
- pastoral referral
- confidence-building session
- learning plan update
