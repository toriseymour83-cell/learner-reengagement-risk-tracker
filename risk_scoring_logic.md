# Risk Scoring Logic

This project uses a simple points-based model to highlight learners who may need additional support.

The score is not designed to make decisions automatically. It is designed to prompt human review.

## Example scoring rules

| Indicator | Points |
|---|---:|
| Attendance below 70% | +3 |
| No work submitted in the last 14 days | +3 |
| No tutor contact in the last 10 days | +2 |
| Previous disengagement concern recorded | +2 |
| Known barrier affecting engagement | +1 |
| Recent positive contact recorded | -2 |

## Risk bands

| Score | Risk level |
|---:|---|
| 0 to 2 | Low |
| 3 to 5 | Medium |
| 6 or more | High |

## Important note

A high score should not be treated as a judgement of the learner.

It should be treated as a prompt for supportive action, such as:

- tutor check-in
- timetable review
- parent/carer contact
- SEND support review
- referral to pastoral support
- adjustment to learning plan
